What YU SLEEP Actually Contains

YU SLEEP lists 9 named ingredients on its label. Here is what each one is included for:

  • Melatonin — A hormone your body naturally produces to signal nighttime; supplementing a small amount helps cue your brain that it's time to wind down and supports a normal sleep schedule.
  • L-Theanine — An amino acid found in green tea that promotes relaxed alertness without drowsiness, helping quiet a busy mind so you can settle into sleep more easily.
  • GABA — A neurotransmitter that dampens excitatory signals in the brain, supporting a calmer state that makes the transition into sleep smoother.
  • Valerian Root — A long-used botanical that may help reduce the time it takes to fall asleep and improve overall sleep quality by soothing the nervous system.
  • Chamomile Extract — A gentle herb traditionally used to calm restlessness and ease tension in the evening, helping create the right internal environment for rest.
  • Passionflower — A botanical that supports a relaxed mood and may help reduce occasional nervousness that keeps you lying awake at night.
  • Lemon Balm — A member of the mint family used to ease mild stress and promote a sense of calm, making it easier to unwind after a long day.
  • Magnesium — An essential mineral involved in muscle relaxation and nerve function; adequate levels support a physically relaxed state conducive to sleep.
