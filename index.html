YU SLEEP is formulated with 9 named ingredients — including Melatonin, L-Theanine, GABA, Valerian Root, Chamomile Extract — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

1

Melatonin

A hormone your body naturally produces to signal nighttime; supplementing a small amount helps cue your brain that it's time to wind down and supports a normal sleep schedule.

2

L-Theanine

An amino acid found in green tea that promotes relaxed alertness without drowsiness, helping quiet a busy mind so you can settle into sleep more easily.

3

GABA

A neurotransmitter that dampens excitatory signals in the brain, supporting a calmer state that makes the transition into sleep smoother.

4

Valerian Root

A long-used botanical that may help reduce the time it takes to fall asleep and improve overall sleep quality by soothing the nervous system.

5

Chamomile Extract

A gentle herb traditionally used to calm restlessness and ease tension in the evening, helping create the right internal environment for rest.

6

Passionflower

A botanical that supports a relaxed mood and may help reduce occasional nervousness that keeps you lying awake at night.

7

Lemon Balm

A member of the mint family used to ease mild stress and promote a sense of calm, making it easier to unwind after a long day.

8

Magnesium

An essential mineral involved in muscle relaxation and nerve function; adequate levels support a physically relaxed state conducive to sleep.

9

Glycine

An amino acid that may help lower core body temperature and improve sleep quality, supporting deeper, more restorative rest.
